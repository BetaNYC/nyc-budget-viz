The NYC Budget Explorer has moved

Every City agency's budget is now in BetaNYC's budget explorer. If this page does not send you there, follow this link:

https://betanyc.github.io/New-York-City-Budget/explorer/#view=agencies

Old links still work: a link to an agency, a unit or a year opens the same agency, unit and year there.

Correction, November 9, 2026: until this date, every year in this explorer was labeled one year late. For example, the FY2025 adopted budget was shown as 2026. The labels are now fixed. What changed
